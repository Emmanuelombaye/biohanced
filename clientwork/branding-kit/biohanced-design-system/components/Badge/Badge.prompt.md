Small status and credential markers used across the catalog.

```jsx
<Badge tone="purity">99.7% purity</Badge>
<Badge tone="save">Save 12%</Badge>
<Badge tone="cat" dotColor="var(--cat-recovery)">Healing &amp; Recovery</Badge>
<Badge tone="count">3</Badge>
```

`purity` is plain green text next to a price; `save` is the rounded pill on bundle cards; `cat` labels a product's category with its taxonomy dot; `count` is the cart chip in the nav.
