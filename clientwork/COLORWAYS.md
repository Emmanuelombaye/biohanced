## Usage rules

1. Production site, email, and packaging default to **Voltage**.
2. Campaign landing pages may swap mark gradient only — do not change Ink/Warm White structure.
3. Never recolor the bolt outside approved colorway gradients.
4. Purity / success UI stays `#1F9E6B` on light cards regardless of colorway.
